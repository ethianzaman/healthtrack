Redesign health reports and add modern date and time pickers

Reports (PDF and Word)
- Blood Pressure Report and Glucose Report each start on their own page
  with a large headline; glucose uses a landscape page for its wide table
- One row per day: morning / evening blood pressure and fasting, before
  and 2h after meal glucose sit side by side on the same row
- A Status column sums up each day with its worst reading (Normal,
  Elevated, High, Low, Danger) as a coloured pill, per ACC/AHA 2017 and
  ADA; out-of-range values are coloured too
- Glucose shown as "5.4 mmol/L or 97 mg/dL", blood pressure with mmHg,
  each with the time beside it in small grey text
- Status legend under each table; medication list on its own page
- Patient details, averages and summary tables removed for a cleaner read
- Choose optional columns before generating (status, time, pulse, arm,
  notes); doctor share links always get every column
- Whole words wrap in PDF tables (no hyphen splits)

Date and time input
- New calendar date picker: one tap selects and closes, Today and Clear
  buttons, Saturday week start, month/year dropdowns for date of birth
- New time picker with hour, minute and AM/PM columns that update the
  value immediately, plus Now and Done
- Both open as a popover on desktop and a bottom sheet on phones
- Used for readings, report dates, date of birth and prescription date
